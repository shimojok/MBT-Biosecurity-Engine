# Supply Chain Resilience and Food Loss Reduction (MBT55)

MBT55 strengthens food supply chain resilience by reducing spoilage,  
stabilizing microbial communities, improving cold chain performance,  
and enhancing storage stability.  
This reduces food loss from farm to retail and increases economic efficiency.

---

# 1. Overview

Food loss occurs at multiple stages:

- Harvest and handling
- Transport
- Storage
- Processing
- Retail display

Drivers include:

- Spoilage microbes
- Temperature fluctuations
- Moisture imbalance
- Oxidation
- Poor sanitation

MBT55 provides a biological stabilization layer across the entire chain.

---

# 2. Reduction of Spoilage Losses

MBT55 reduces:

- Mold growth
- Soft rot
- Surface contamination
- Fermentation spoilage

Mechanisms:

- Organic acids
- Antagonism
- Niche exclusion
- Redox stabilization

---

# 3. Improved Cold Chain Stability

MBT55 enhances:

- Temperature tolerance
- Moisture balance
- Oxidation resistance
- Microbial stability

This reduces losses during transport and storage.

---

# 4. Storage Optimization

MBT55 improves:

- Grain storage stability
- Fresh produce hydration
- Meat and fish freshness
- Dairy microbial balance

---

# 5. Retail Shelf Stability

MBT55 extends:

- Fresh produce shelf life
- Meat color stability
- Aroma retention
- Texture quality

This reduces retail waste.

---

# 6. Economic Impact

MBT55 reduces:

- Postharvest loss (10–40%)
- Cold chain waste
- Retail shrinkage
- Quality downgrade losses

---

# 7. PBPE Integration

Supply chain resilience becomes PBPE value:

- SRC（Spoilage Reduction Credit）
- QPC（Quality Premium Credit）
- FSC（Food Safety Credit）

---

# 8. One-Sentence Summary

**MBT55 strengthens supply chain resilience by reducing spoilage,  
improving cold chain stability, and preserving quality from farm to retail.**
