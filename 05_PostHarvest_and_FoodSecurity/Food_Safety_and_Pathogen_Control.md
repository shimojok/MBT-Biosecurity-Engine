# Food Safety and Pathogen Control (MBT55)

MBT55 enhances food safety by suppressing pathogens, stabilizing surface  
microbiomes, reducing spoilage organisms, and improving postharvest hygiene.  
This reduces foodborne illness risk and improves supply chain reliability.

---

# 1. Overview

Food contamination is driven by:

- Pathogenic bacteria
- Fungal spores
- Poor sanitation
- Temperature fluctuations
- High moisture
- Postharvest handling stress

MBT55 provides a biological safety layer across the entire food chain.

---

# 2. Pathogen Suppression

MBT55 suppresses:

- Salmonella
- E. coli
- Listeria
- Campylobacter
- Staphylococcus
- Fungal pathogens

Mechanisms:

- Organic acids
- Antagonism
- Niche exclusion
- Redox stabilization

---

# 3. Surface Microbiome Stabilization

MBT55 forms a protective microbial layer on:

- Fruits
- Vegetables
- Meat surfaces
- Eggs
- Storage containers

Benefits:

- Reduced pathogen attachment
- Lower contamination risk
- Improved hygiene stability

---

# 4. Mycotoxin Reduction

MBT55 reduces:

- Aflatoxin
- Ochratoxin
- Fumonisin

Mechanisms:

- Fungal suppression
- Redox disruption
- Competitive exclusion

---

# 5. Water and Equipment Sanitation

MBT55 improves:

- Wash water quality
- Processing line hygiene
- Storage tank stability

This reduces cross-contamination.

---

# 6. PBPE Integration

Food safety improvements become PBPE value:

- FSC（Food Safety Credit）
- SRC（Spoilage Reduction Credit）
- QPC（Quality Premium Credit）

---

**MBT55 enhances food safety by suppressing pathogens, stabilizing surface  
microbiomes, reducing mycotoxins, and improving sanitation across the food chain.**
