# Banana Disease Matrix × MBT55

Bananas face some of the most severe plant diseases globally,  
especially Fusarium TR4, Black Sigatoka, and viral infections.  
These diseases threaten global supply chains and farmer livelihoods.

MBT55 provides a multi-layered biosecurity system that suppresses pathogens,  
regenerates soil, and strengthens plant immunity.

---

# 1. Major Banana Diseases

## 1.1 Fusarium Wilt TR4 (Panama Disease)
- Most destructive banana disease  
- Soil-borne, long-term persistence  
- Causes vascular collapse and plant death  
- No effective chemical cure  

## 1.2 Black Sigatoka (Mycosphaerella fijiensis)
- Leaf necrosis  
- Reduced photosynthesis  
- Lower yield and fruit quality  

## 1.3 Banana Bunchy Top Virus (BBTV)
- Stunted growth  
- Deformed leaves  
- Severe yield loss  

## 1.4 Bacterial Wilt (Xanthomonas)
- Fruit rot  
- Plant collapse  
- Rapid spread

# 2. MBT55 Mechanisms for Banana Biosecurity

## 2.1 Organic Acid Suppression
- Inhibits Fusarium TR4 activity  
- Reduces Sigatoka spore germination  
- Stabilizes leaf surface microbiome  

## 2.2 Soil Regeneration
- Improves soil structure  
- Enhances root health  
- Reduces TR4 persistence  

## 2.3 Niche Exclusion
- MBT55 colonizes root and leaf surfaces  
- Blocks pathogen attachment  

## 2.4 ISR Activation
- Strengthens systemic resistance  
- Improves tolerance to TR4 and Sigatoka  

## 2.5 Redox Cycling
- Reduces oxidative stress  
- Improves plant vitality
