# Rice Disease Matrix × MBT55

Rice is the world’s most important staple crop and is highly vulnerable  
to fungal, bacterial, and viral diseases.  
Blast, sheath blight, and bacterial blight cause major yield losses  
and threaten food security across Asia, Africa, and Latin America.

MBT55 provides a scalable biosecurity solution that stabilizes rice ecosystems  
through microbial balance, organic acids, and ISR activation.

---

# 1. Major Rice Diseases

## 1.1 Rice Blast (Magnaporthe oryzae)
- Most destructive rice disease  
- Affects leaves, stems, and panicles  
- Causes severe yield loss  

## 1.2 Bacterial Blight (Xanthomonas oryzae)
- Leaf blight  
- Rapid spread under humidity  
- Reduces grain filling  

## 1.3 Sheath Blight (Rhizoctonia solani)
- Affects stems and lower canopy  
- Reduces photosynthesis  
- Increases lodging  

## 1.4 Tungro Virus
- Vector-borne  
- Stunted growth  
- Reduced tillering

# 2. MBT55 Mechanisms for Rice Biosecurity

## 2.1 Organic Acid Barrier
- Inhibits fungal germination  
- Reduces blast severity  
- Stabilizes leaf surface microbiome  

## 2.2 Niche Exclusion
- MBT55 colonizes leaf and root surfaces  
- Blocks pathogen attachment  

## 2.3 ISR Activation
- Enhances resistance to blast and blight  
- Improves stress tolerance  

## 2.4 Soil Regeneration
- Improves root health  
- Enhances nutrient uptake  
- Reduces sheath blight severity  

## 2.5 Redox Cycling
- Reduces oxidative stress  
- Improves grain quality
