# ISR and Plant Immunity (MBT55)

MBT55 activates plant immune systems through ISR (Induced Systemic Resistance),  
microbial signaling molecules, SCFA pathways, and root-surface colonization.  
This strengthens plant defenses against pathogens and environmental stress.

---

# 1. What Is ISR?

ISR is a plant-wide immune activation triggered by beneficial microbes.  
It enhances resistance to:

- Fungal pathogens  
- Bacterial infections  
- Viral stress  
- Abiotic stress (heat, drought, salinity)

ISR is a **non-chemical, systemic immunity upgrade**.

---

# 2. How MBT55 Activates ISR

MBT55 triggers ISR through:

## 2.1 Microbial Signaling Molecules
- Lipopeptides  
- Volatile organic compounds (VOCs)  
- SCFA  
- Phenolics  

These activate plant defense pathways.

---

## 2.2 Root Colonization
MBT55 forms stable colonies on:

- Root surfaces  
- Rhizosphere soil  
- Root hairs  

This creates continuous immune stimulation.

---

## 2.3 Organic Acid Pathways
SCFA and organic acids:

- Strengthen root cell walls  
- Improve nutrient uptake  
- Reduce pathogen penetration  

---

## 2.4 Redox and Mineral Signals
Mn/Fe redox cycling produces:

- Electron signals  
- Oxidative cues  
- Stress-response triggers  

These enhance plant resilience.
