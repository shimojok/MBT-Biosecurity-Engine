# One Health Integration (MBT55)

MBT55 enhances One Health by improving microbial stability across  
plants, animals, humans, soil, and water systems.  
It reduces pathogen load, strengthens immunity, and lowers AMR risk  
through ecological and metabolic mechanisms.

---

# 1. What Is One Health?

One Health recognizes the interconnected health of:

- Humans  
- Animals  
- Plants  
- Ecosystems  

MBT55 supports all four domains simultaneously.
