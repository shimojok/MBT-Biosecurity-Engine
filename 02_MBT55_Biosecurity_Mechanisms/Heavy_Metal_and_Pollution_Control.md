# Heavy Metal and Pollution Control (MBT55)

MBT55 reduces heavy metals, chemical pollutants, and toxic compounds  
through microbial transformation, mineral chelation, redox cycling,  
and humification pathways.  
This mechanism restores soil, water, and plant safety while improving  
ecosystem resilience.

---

# 1. Overview

Pollution sources addressed by MBT55:

- Heavy metals (Cd, Pb, Hg, As, Cr)
- Agricultural chemicals
- Industrial pollutants
- Organic toxins
- Ammonia and sulfides
- Excess nitrates and phosphates

MBT55 neutralizes these through biological and mineral processes.

---

# 2. Chelation and Immobilization

MBT55 produces:

- Fulvic acids  
- Humic substances  
- Organic acids  
- Phenolic compounds  

These bind to heavy metals and immobilize them.

Effects:

- Reduced plant uptake  
- Lower toxicity  
- Improved soil safety  

---

# 3. Redox Transformation

Redox-active microbes drive:

- Mn/Fe cycling  
- Electron transfer  
- Oxidation–reduction of metals  

Examples:

- Cr(VI) → Cr(III) (less toxic)
- As(V) → As(III) immobilization
- Hg methylation suppression

This reduces environmental toxicity.

---

# 4. Microbial Degradation of Organic Pollutants

MBT55 decomposes:

- Pesticide residues  
- Hydrocarbons  
- Phenolic compounds  
- Ammonia and sulfides  

Mechanisms:

- Enzymatic breakdown  
- Anaerobic fermentation  
- Oxidative degradation  

---

# 5. Humification and Long-Term Stabilization

Humification converts pollutants into:

- Stable carbon complexes  
- Non-toxic organic matter  

This provides long-term pollution control.

---

# 6. Water System Purification

MBT55 reduces:

- COD  
- BOD  
- Algae growth  
- Odor compounds  
- Suspended solids  

Applications:

- Irrigation water  
- Aquaculture  
- Wastewater treatment  

---
