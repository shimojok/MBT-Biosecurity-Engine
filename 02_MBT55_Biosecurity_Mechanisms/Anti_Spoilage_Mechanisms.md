# Anti-Spoilage Mechanisms (MBT55)

MBT55 prevents spoilage by stabilizing microbial communities, suppressing  
oxidation, reducing pathogenic load, and creating biochemical conditions  
that inhibit deterioration.  
These mechanisms dramatically reduce post-harvest loss and extend shelf life.

---

# 1. Overview

Spoilage is driven by:

- Oxidative stress  
- Pathogenic microbes  
- Enzymatic degradation  
- Moisture imbalance  
- Temperature fluctuations  

MBT55 counteracts all major spoilage pathways simultaneously.

---

# 2. Organic Acid Shield

MBT55 produces:

- Lactic acid  
- Acetic acid  
- Propionic acid  
- Butyric acid (SCFA)

Effects:

- Lowers pH  
- Inhibits spoilage bacteria  
- Blocks fungal growth  
- Stabilizes surface microbiota  

This is the primary anti-spoilage barrier.

- Quinones

- Redox-active metabolites

- Fruits

- Tissue breakdown
