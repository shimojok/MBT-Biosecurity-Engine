# AMR Reduction and Antibiotic Sparing (MBT55)

MBT55 reduces antimicrobial resistance (AMR) risk by lowering pathogen load,  
improving gut immunity, stabilizing microbial communities, and reducing  
the need for antibiotics in livestock systems.  
This is a core component of MBT55’s One Health biosecurity impact.

---

# 1. Overview

AMR is driven by:

- Overuse of antibiotics
- High pathogen pressure
- Poor gut microbiome stability
- Stress and transport
- Contaminated water and feed

MBT55 addresses all major AMR drivers simultaneously.

---

# 2. Pathogen Load Reduction

MBT55 suppresses:

- E. coli
- Salmonella
- Clostridium
- Campylobacter

Mechanisms:

- Organic acids
- Antagonism
- Niche exclusion
- Redox stabilization

Lower pathogens → lower antibiotic need.

---

# 3. Immune Enhancement

SCFA and microbial signals activate:

- GALT (gut-associated lymphoid tissue)
- Macrophages
- Neutrophils
- T-reg cells

Stronger immunity reduces infection frequency.

---

# 4. Microbiome Stabilization

MBT55 restores:

- Aerobic–anaerobic balance
- SCFA pathways
- Fiber fermentation
- Redox cycling

Stable microbiomes resist pathogen invasion.

---

# 5. Antibiotic Sparing Effect

With MBT55:

- Fewer infections  
- Faster recovery  
- Lower mortality  
- Reduced antibiotic dependency  

This directly reduces AMR selection pressure.

---

# 6. Environmental AMR Reduction

MBT55 reduces AMR spread in:

- Manure  
- Water systems  
- Soil  
- Housing environments  

By lowering pathogen load and stabilizing microbial communities.

---

# 7. PBPE Integration

AMR reduction becomes PBPE value:

- AMRC（AMR Reduction Credit）
- LHC（Livestock Health Credit）
- WQC（Water Quality Credit）

---

# 8. One-Sentence Summary

**MBT55 reduces AMR risk by lowering pathogen load, strengthening immunity,  
stabilizing the gut microbiome, and reducing antibiotic dependency.**
