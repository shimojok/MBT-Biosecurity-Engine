# Livestock Pathogen Suppression (MBT55)

MBT55 suppresses livestock pathogens through organic acids, antagonism,  
niche exclusion, redox stabilization, and SCFA-driven immune activation.  
This reduces disease incidence, antibiotic dependency, and AMR risk  
across cattle, poultry, swine, goats, and aquaculture.

---

# 1. Overview

Livestock pathogens thrive under:

- High-density housing
- Stress and transport
- Poor feed quality
- Antibiotic overuse
- Gut microbiome imbalance

MBT55 restores microbial stability and blocks pathogen expansion.

---

# 2. Organic Acid Suppression

MBT55 produces:

- Lactic acid  
- Acetic acid  
- Propionic acid  
- Butyric acid  

Effects:

- Lowers gut pH  
- Disrupts pathogen membranes  
- Inhibits Salmonella, E. coli, Clostridium  
- Stabilizes fermentation  

---

# 3. Antagonism

MBT55 microbes produce:

- Antimicrobial peptides  
- Redox-active metabolites  
- Competitive inhibitors  

These directly suppress harmful bacteria and fungi.

---

# 4. Niche Exclusion

MBT55 occupies:

- Gut surfaces  
- Feed particles  
- Mucosal layers  

This blocks pathogen attachment and colonization.

---

# 5. Immune Activation

SCFA and microbial signals activate:

- GALT (gut-associated lymphoid tissue)
- Macrophages
- Neutrophils
- T-reg cells

This strengthens systemic immunity.

---

# 6. Aquaculture Pathogen Control

MBT55 reduces:

- Vibrio  
- Aeromonas  
- Fungal spores  

Mechanisms:

- Water microbiome stabilization  
- Organic acid diffusion  
- Redox balance  

---

**MBT55 suppresses livestock pathogens through organic acids, antagonism,
