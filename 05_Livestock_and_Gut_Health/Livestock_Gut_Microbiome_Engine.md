# Livestock Gut Microbiome Engine (MBT55)

The Livestock Gut Microbiome Engine explains how MBT55 stabilizes  
the digestive ecosystem of cattle, poultry, swine, goats, and aquaculture species.  
It enhances fermentation, reduces pathogens, improves feed efficiency,  
and strengthens immunity through SCFA-driven metabolic pathways.

---

# 1. Overview

Livestock gut instability is caused by:

- High-grain diets
- Antibiotic overuse
- Pathogen pressure
- Stress and transport
- Poor-quality feed
- Mycotoxins

MBT55 restores microbial balance and improves digestive performance.

---

# 2. Microbial Stabilization

MBT55 introduces:

- Lactic acid bacteria
- Yeasts
- SCFA producers
- Fiber-degrading microbes
- Redox-active bacteria

Benefits:

- Reduced diarrhea
- Improved digestion
- Lower pathogen load

---

# 3. Fermentation Optimization

MBT55 enhances:

- Fiber breakdown
- Volatile fatty acid (VFA) production
- Protein utilization
- Energy extraction

This increases **feed conversion efficiency (FCE)**.

---

# 4. Pathogen Suppression

MBT55 suppresses:

- E. coli
- Salmonella
- Clostridium
- Campylobacter

Mechanisms:

- Organic acids
- Antagonism
- Niche exclusion

---

# 5. Methane Reduction (Ruminants)

MBT55 reduces CH₄ by:

- Shifting fermentation toward SCFA
- Reducing hydrogen availability
- Supporting methanotrophs

This directly contributes to **PBPE-MRC**.

---

# 6. Immune Enhancement

MBT55 improves:

- Gut barrier integrity
- Immune cell activation
- Stress tolerance

---

# 7. PBPE Integration

Livestock gut improvements become PBPE value:

- LHC（Livestock Health Credit）
- MRC（Methane Reduction Credit）
- FEC（Feed Efficiency Credit）

---

# 8. One-Sentence Summary

**MBT55 stabilizes the livestock gut microbiome, improving digestion,  
reducing pathogens, enhancing immunity, and lowering methane emissions.**
