# SCFA and Immune Modulation (MBT55)

MBT55 enhances SCFA (short-chain fatty acid) production in both livestock  
and humans, improving gut health, immune activation, and metabolic stability.  
SCFA are central to MBT55’s One Health biosecurity function.

---

# 1. What Are SCFA?

Key SCFA:

- Acetate
- Propionate
- Butyrate

Produced by microbial fermentation of:

- Fiber
- Resistant starch
- Organic matter

---

# 2. SCFA Functions in Livestock

SCFA improve:

- Gut barrier integrity
- Nutrient absorption
- Immune activation
- Anti-inflammatory response
- Energy metabolism

---

# 3. MBT55 Mechanisms for SCFA Enhancement

MBT55 increases SCFA by:

- Boosting fermentative microbes
- Enhancing fiber degradation
- Reducing pathogenic competition
- Stabilizing pH
- Improving redox balance

# 4. Immune Modulation

- T-reg cells
- Macrophages
- Neutrophils
- Gut-associated lymphoid tissue (GALT)

Benefits:

- Lower inflammation
- Stronger disease resistance
- Improved vaccine response
