# One Health Livestock Integration (MBT55)

MBT55 integrates livestock health, environmental stability, and human safety  
into a unified One Health framework.  
By improving gut health, reducing pathogens, lowering AMR, and stabilizing  
soil–water ecosystems, MBT55 creates cross-domain biosecurity benefits.

---

# 1. What Is One Health?

One Health recognizes the interconnected health of:

- Animals  
- Humans  
- Plants  
- Soil  
- Water  
- Climate  

MBT55 strengthens all domains simultaneously.

---

# 2. Livestock Health Improvements

MBT55 enhances:

- Gut microbiome stability  
- SCFA production  
- Immune activation  
- Feed efficiency  
- Stress tolerance  

This reduces disease and improves productivity.

---

# 3. Reduction of Zoonotic Pathogens

MBT55 suppresses:

- Salmonella  
- E. coli  
- Campylobacter  
- Listeria  

This reduces foodborne and zoonotic risk.

---

# 4. AMR Risk Reduction

MBT55 lowers:

- Antibiotic use  
- Pathogen load  
- Environmental AMR spread  

This is critical for global health.

---

# 5. Environmental Integration

MBT55 improves:

- Soil carbon  
- Water quality  
- Odor reduction  
- Waste decomposition  

Healthy environments → healthy animals → healthy humans.

---

# 6. Food Chain Benefits

MBT55 improves:

- Meat quality  
- Milk stability  
- Egg safety  
- Aquaculture health  

This enhances food safety and nutritional value.

---

# 7. PBPE Integration

One Health livestock outputs become PBPE value:

- LHC（Livestock Health Credit）
- AMRC（AMR Reduction Credit）
- WQC（Water Quality Credit）
- MRC（Methane Reduction Credit）

---

# 8. One-Sentence Summary

**MBT55 integrates livestock, environmental, and human health into a unified  
One Health system by reducing pathogens, lowering AMR, improving gut stability,  
and enhancing soil–water ecosystems.**
