# Cacao Case Study  
### MBT55 × PBPE: Suppression of Black Pod Disease, Fermentation Optimization, and Flavor Enhancement in Cacao Production

---

## 1. Background: Cacao Production Under Severe Biological Pressure

Cacao production faces major biological and climate threats:

### **Primary Diseases**
- **Black Pod (Phytophthora palmivora / megakarya)**  
- **Frosty Pod Rot (Moniliophthora roreri)**  
- **Witches’ Broom (Moniliophthora perniciosa)**  

Black Pod alone causes:

- **20–40% annual yield losses**  
- Rapid fruit rot  
- Massive economic losses in West Africa, Latin America, and Southeast Asia  

Chemical fungicides are costly, inconsistent, and environmentally damaging.  
A biological, regenerative solution is required.

MBT55 stabilizes:

**Result: Reduced fruit drop and improved pod health.**
