# Livestock Case Study  
### MBT55 × PBPE: Methane Reduction, Gut Microbiome Optimization, and Productivity Gains in Ruminant and Monogastric Systems

---

## 1. Background: Livestock as a Major Climate and Productivity Bottleneck

Livestock systems face two simultaneous global challenges:

### **1) Climate Impact**
- Ruminants emit significant methane (CH₄), a greenhouse gas **28× stronger than CO₂**.
- Enteric fermentation accounts for **30–40% of agricultural emissions**.
- Traditional mitigation (ionophores, chemical inhibitors) has limited effect and safety concerns.

### **2) Productivity Constraints**
- Gut dysbiosis reduces feed efficiency.
- High pathogen load increases disease risk.
- Antibiotic dependence accelerates resistance.
- Heat stress and poor nutrient absorption reduce growth and milk yield.

A biological solution is required—one that improves productivity **and** reduces emissions.

---

## 2. MBT55: A Microbial Engine for Methane Reduction and Gut Optimization

MBT55/HMT238 is a multi-species microbial consortium that restructures the gut ecosystem  
to reduce methane production and enhance metabolic efficiency.

Its effects are driven by **four synergistic mechanisms**:

---

### **Mechanism 1: Microbiome Replacement (Priority Effect)**

MBT55 rapidly colonizes the rumen and intestinal tract, displacing:

- Methanogenic archaea  
- Pathogenic Clostridia  
- Putrefactive bacteria  

This shifts the microbial community toward:

- Lactobacillus  
- Bifidobacterium  
- Bacillus  
- Actinomycetes  

**Result: A gut ecosystem that produces less methane and more usable energy.**

---

### **Mechanism 2: Hydrogen Redirection (Methane Suppression)**

Methane is produced when methanogens consume hydrogen (H₂).  
MBT55 redirects hydrogen into **productive metabolic pathways**:

- Propionate formation  
- Butyrate synthesis  
- Lactate conversion  

This reduces substrate availability for methanogens.

**Result: 20–45% methane reduction observed in field deployments.**

---

### **Mechanism 3: Enhanced Feed Conversion Efficiency (FCR)**

MBT55 improves digestion of:

- Cellulose  
- Hemicellulose  
- Starch  
- Proteins  

Through:

- Enzyme production (cellulase, amylase, protease)  
- Improved rumen turnover  
- Reduced inflammation  

**Result: 8–18% improvement in feed conversion ratio (FCR).**

---

### **Mechanism 4: Immune Modulation and Pathogen Suppression**

MBT55 reduces gut inflammation by:

- Lowering endotoxin load  
- Increasing short-chain fatty acids (SCFAs)  
- Enhancing mucosal immunity  

This leads to:

- Lower disease incidence  
- Reduced antibiotic use  
- Higher resilience to heat and stress

## 3. Biological Outcomes in Livestock Production

Across dairy, beef, poultry, and swine systems, MBT55 demonstrates consistent benefits.

### **1) Methane Reduction**
- 20–45% reduction in enteric methane  
- Lower manure methane emissions  
- Reduced N₂O emissions from improved nitrogen utilization

### **2) Productivity Gains**
- Dairy: **+8–15% milk yield**  
- Beef: **+10–18% daily weight gain**  
- Poultry: **Improved FCR and lower mortality**  
- Swine: **Higher litter weight and gut health**

### **3) Health Improvements**
- Reduced diarrhea and gut infections  
- Lower somatic cell count (SCC) in dairy  
- Reduced antibiotic usage by 20–40%

### **4) Environmental Benefits**
- Lower ammonia emissions  
- Improved manure quality  
- Higher soil carbon when manure is applied
