## 1. Background: Global Beekeeping Under Biological Stress

Honeybee colonies face increasing biological threats that reduce productivity and  
destabilize pollination systems.

### **Major Diseases**
- **Chalkbrood (Ascosphaera apis)**  
- **Blue Mold contamination (Penicillium spp.)**  
- **Nosema spp.**  
- **Secondary fungal and bacterial infections**  

Chalkbrood alone causes:

- **Larval mortality**  
- **Colony weakening**  
- **Reduced honey production**  
- **Lower pollination efficiency**  

Chemical treatments are limited, often ineffective, and can disrupt the hive microbiome.

A biological, safe, and colony-compatible solution is required.
