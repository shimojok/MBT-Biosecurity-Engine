# Soil Microbiome Stability (MBT55)

MBT55 stabilizes the soil microbiome by restoring microbial diversity,  
balancing aerobic–anaerobic ratios, suppressing pathogens, and enhancing  
functional metabolic pathways.  
This creates a resilient soil ecosystem capable of supporting high yields  
and long-term fertility.

---

# 1. Overview

Soil microbiome collapse is caused by:

- Chemical overuse
- Monoculture
- Erosion
- Organic matter loss
- Pathogen accumulation
- Climate stress

MBT55 rebuilds microbial networks and restores ecological balance.

---

# 2. Microbial Diversity Restoration

MBT55 introduces:

- Aerobic bacteria
- Anaerobic bacteria
- Yeasts
- Fungi
- Actinomycetes
- Redox-active microbes

This re-establishes a **multi-kingdom ecosystem**.

---

# 3. Aerobic–Anaerobic Balance

Natural soils often shift toward anaerobic dominance under stress.  
MBT55 restores the engineered **55:45 aerobic–anaerobic ratio**, enabling:

- Faster decomposition
- Stronger pathogen suppression
- Higher SCFA production
- Improved nutrient cycling

---

# 4. Pathogen Suppression

MBT55 suppresses:

- Fusarium
- Pythium
- Phytophthora
- Rhizoctonia

Mechanisms:

- Organic acids
- Antagonism
- Niche exclusion

---

# 5. Functional Stability

MBT55 enhances:

- SCFA pathways
- Redox cycling
- Humification
- Nitrogen retention
- Carbon stabilization

These functions create **long-term soil resilience**.

---

# 6. Microbial Network Reinforcement

MBT55 strengthens:

- Rhizosphere interactions
- Root exudate utilization
- Mycorrhizal synergy
- Biofilm formation

This improves plant–soil communication.
