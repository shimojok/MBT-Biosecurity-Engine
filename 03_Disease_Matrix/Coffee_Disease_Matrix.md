# Coffee Disease Matrix × MBT55

Coffee is one of the most disease‑vulnerable crops in the world.  
Climate change is accelerating fungal, bacterial, and pest‑driven diseases,  
causing yield loss, quality decline, and price volatility.

MBT55 provides a multi‑mechanism biosecurity solution that stabilizes  
coffee production and improves quality.

---

# 1. Major Coffee Diseases

## 1.1 Coffee Leaf Rust (Hemileia vastatrix)
- Global yield loss: 20–50%  
- Increased under high humidity and temperature  
- Fungicide resistance rising  

## 1.2 Coffee Berry Disease (Colletotrichum kahawae)
- Affects cherries directly  
- Causes premature drop and rot  

## 1.3 Root‑Knot Nematodes
- Damage root systems  
- Reduce nutrient uptake  
- Increase drought sensitivity  

## 1.4 Coffee Wilt Disease
- Caused by Fusarium xylarioides  
- Rapid tree decline  

## 1.5 Black Rot / Anthracnose
- Affects leaves and berries  
- Reduces quality and aroma

# 2. MBT55 Mechanisms for Coffee Biosecurity

## 2.1 Antagonism and Organic Acids
- Suppresses rust spores  
- Reduces fungal germination  
- Stabilizes leaf surface microbiome  

## 2.2 Niche Exclusion
- MBT55 colonizes leaf and root surfaces  
- Blocks pathogen attachment  
- Reduces nematode activity  

## 2.3 ISR Activation
- Strengthens plant immunity  
- Reduces rust severity  
- Improves drought tolerance  

## 2.4 Soil Regeneration
- Increases organic matter  
- Improves root health  
- Enhances nutrient uptake  

## 2.5 Anti‑Spoilage Mechanisms
- Reduces cherry rot  
- Improves post‑harvest stability
