# Grape Disease Matrix × MBT55

Grapes are highly sensitive to fungal and bacterial diseases,  
especially under rising humidity and temperature.  
Downy mildew, powdery mildew, and botrytis cause major economic losses  
in wine, table grape, and raisin industries.

MBT55 provides a biological defense system that stabilizes vineyard health  
and improves fruit quality.

---

# 1. Major Grape Diseases

## 1.1 Downy Mildew (Plasmopara viticola)
- Rapid spread under humidity  
- Leaf yellowing and necrosis  
- Severe yield loss  

## 1.2 Powdery Mildew (Erysiphe necator)
- White fungal growth  
- Affects leaves and berries  
- Reduces sugar accumulation  

## 1.3 Botrytis / Gray Mold
- Affects clusters  
- Causes rot and quality loss  
- Major issue in storage  

## 1.4 Black Rot (Guignardia)
- Leaf and berry lesions  
- Reduces marketability  

## 1.5 Pierce’s Disease (Xylella fastidiosa)
- Vector-borne  
- Causes vine decline

# 2. MBT55 Mechanisms for Grape Biosecurity

## 2.1 Organic Acid Barrier
- Inhibits fungal germination  
- Reduces mildew severity  
- Stabilizes leaf surface microbiome  

## 2.2 Niche Exclusion
- MBT55 occupies leaf and berry surfaces  
- Blocks pathogen attachment  
- Reduces botrytis spread  

## 2.3 Anti-Spoilage Mechanisms
- Prevents berry rot  
- Reduces post-harvest losses  

## 2.4 ISR Activation
- Enhances resistance to mildew  
- Improves stress tolerance  

## 2.5 Soil Regeneration
- Strengthens root systems  
- Improves nutrient uptake  
- Enhances vine vigor
