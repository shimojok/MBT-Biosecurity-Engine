# Citrus Disease Matrix × MBT55

Citrus crops face severe biological threats worldwide, especially under  
climate-driven pathogen expansion.  
HLB (citrus greening), canker, and fungal diseases are destabilizing yields  
and threatening global citrus supply chains.

MBT55 provides a multi-layered biosecurity solution that suppresses pathogens,  
regenerates soil, and strengthens plant immunity.

---

# 1. Major Citrus Diseases

## 1.1 HLB – Citrus Greening (Candidatus Liberibacter)
- Most destructive citrus disease globally  
- Spread by psyllid vectors  
- Causes fruit deformation, bitterness, tree decline  
- No effective chemical cure  

## 1.2 Citrus Canker (Xanthomonas)
- Leaf and fruit lesions  
- High spread rate under humidity  
- Causes premature fruit drop  

## 1.3 Phytophthora Root Rot
- Soil-borne  
- Weakens root systems  
- Reduces nutrient uptake  

## 1.4 Greasy Spot (Mycosphaerella)
- Leaf infection  
- Reduces photosynthesis  
- Increases defoliation  

## 1.5 Melanose (Diaporthe)
- Affects fruit surface  
- Reduces market quality

# 2. MBT55 Mechanisms for Citrus Biosecurity

## 2.1 Organic Acid Suppression
- Reduces bacterial load (HLB-associated bacteria)  
- Inhibits fungal germination  
- Stabilizes leaf surface microbiome  

## 2.2 Niche Exclusion
- MBT55 colonizes leaf and root surfaces  
- Blocks pathogen attachment  
- Reduces canker spread  

## 2.3 ISR Activation
- Enhances systemic resistance  
- Strengthens defense against HLB and canker  
- Improves drought and heat tolerance  

## 2.4 Soil Regeneration
- Improves root health  
- Enhances nutrient uptake  
- Reduces Phytophthora severity  

## 2.5 Redox Cycling
- Reduces oxidative stress  
- Improves tree vitality
