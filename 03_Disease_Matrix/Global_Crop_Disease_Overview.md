# Global Crop Disease Overview

Global crop diseases are accelerating due to climate change,  
pathogen migration, soil degradation, and increased monoculture.  
These diseases reduce yield, destabilize markets, and threaten global food security.

MBT55 provides a scalable biosecurity solution by suppressing pathogens,  
stabilizing soil, and activating plant immunity.

---

# 1. Key Drivers of Disease Expansion

- Rising temperature and humidity  
- Increased rainfall variability  
- Soil microbiome collapse  
- Globalized trade and pathogen movement  
- Pesticide resistance  
- Monoculture and genetic uniformity  

These factors create ideal conditions for disease outbreaks.

---

# 2. Major Global Crop Diseases

## 2.1 Fungal Diseases
- Rust (coffee, wheat, soybean)  
- Powdery mildew (grapes, cucurbits)  
- Downy mildew (grapes, lettuce)  
- Blight (potato, tomato)  
- Anthracnose (mango, tea)  

## 2.2 Bacterial Diseases
- HLB (citrus greening)  
- Xylella fastidiosa (olive, grape)  
- Bacterial wilt (solanaceae)  

## 2.3 Viral Diseases
- Mosaic viruses  
- Leaf curl viruses  
- Banana bunchy top virus  

## 2.4 Soil‑Borne Diseases
- Fusarium  
- Pythium  
- Phytophthora  
- Rhizoctonia
