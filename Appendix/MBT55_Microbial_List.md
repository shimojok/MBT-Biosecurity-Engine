# MBT55 Microbial List  
### Comprehensive Composition, Functional Groups, and Metabolic Architecture of MBT55/HMT238

---

## 1. Overview

MBT55/HMT238 is a **multi-species microbial consortium** composed of  
**120+ aerobic, anaerobic, and facultative anaerobic microorganisms**.

It is engineered through ecological selection rather than genetic modification,  
forming a **self-organizing metabolic network** capable of:

- Rapid decomposition of organic matter  
- Pathogen suppression  
- Soil regeneration  
- Carbon sequestration  
- Immune activation in plants and animals  
- Micro-environment control  

### **Aerobic : Anaerobic Ratio**
- **55% Aerobic microbes**  
- **45% Anaerobic microbes**

### **Four Primary Decomposer Groups**
1. **Starch-degrading bacteria**  
2. **Protein-degrading bacteria**  
3. **Lipid-degrading bacteria**  
4. **Cellulose-degrading bacteria**

These groups form the metabolic backbone of MBT55.
