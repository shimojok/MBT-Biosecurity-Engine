# Water Quality and Hydration Dynamics (MBT55)

MBT55 improves water quality and hydration dynamics by reducing organic load,  
suppressing algae, stabilizing microbial communities, and enhancing soil  
water retention.  
This creates healthier irrigation systems, more resilient crops,  
and improved ecosystem stability.

---

# 1. Overview

Water degradation is driven by:

- High organic load (COD/BOD)
- Algae blooms
- Pathogenic contamination
- Sediment accumulation
- Chemical residues
- Low oxygen levels

MBT55 restores water quality through microbial and redox-driven processes.

---

# 2. Organic Load Reduction

MBT55 accelerates decomposition of:

- Organic waste
- Manure runoff
- Plant residues
- Suspended solids

Effects:

- Lower COD/BOD
- Clearer water
- Reduced odor

---

# 3. Algae Suppression

MBT55 reduces algae by:

- Stabilizing nutrient cycles
- Lowering nitrate/phosphate spikes
- Increasing microbial competition
- Improving redox balance

This prevents harmful blooms.

---

# 4. Pathogen Reduction

MBT55 suppresses:

- E. coli
- Salmonella
- Vibrio
- Fungal spores

Mechanisms:

- Organic acids
- Antagonism
- Niche exclusion

---

# 5. Hydration Dynamics in Soil

MBT55 improves soil hydration by:

- Increasing humic substances
- Enhancing aggregation
- Improving porosity
- Increasing water-holding capacity

Benefits:

- Reduced drought stress
- More efficient irrigation
- Higher nutrient mobility

---

# 6. Water System Applications

MBT55 is effective in:

- Irrigation canals
- Aquaculture ponds
- Hydroponics
- Wastewater treatment
- Rainwater storage

---
