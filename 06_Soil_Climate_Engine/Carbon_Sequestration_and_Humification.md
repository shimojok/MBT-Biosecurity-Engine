# Carbon Sequestration and Humification (MBT55)

MBT55 accelerates carbon sequestration by converting organic matter into  
stable humic substances through microbial metabolism, organic acids,  
and redox-driven polymerization.  
This process increases soil carbon, improves fertility, and reduces  
atmospheric CO₂.

---

# 1. Overview

Carbon sequestration requires:

- Organic matter input  
- Microbial transformation  
- Humification  
- Stabilization in soil aggregates  

MBT55 enhances all four stages simultaneously.

---

# 2. Organic Acid Pathways

MBT55 produces:

- Lactic acid  
- Acetic acid  
- Propionic acid  
- Butyric acid  

These acids:

- Break down complex organics  
- Initiate humification  
- Bind minerals  
- Suppress pathogens  

---

# 3. Humification Acceleration

MBT55 converts organic matter into:

- Fulvic acids  
- Humic acids  
- Stable carbon complexes  

Benefits:

- Long-term carbon storage  
- Improved soil structure  
- Higher nutrient retention  

---

# 4. Redox Cycling and Polymerization

Redox-active microbes drive:

- Mn/Fe cycling  
- Quinone polymerization  
- Electron shuttling  

These reactions stabilize carbon into **recalcitrant forms**.

---

# 5. Soil Carbon Stabilization

MBT55 increases:

- Microaggregate formation  
- Mineral-associated organic carbon  
- Water-stable aggregates  

This locks carbon into the soil for decades.

---

# 6. Climate Impact

MBT55 reduces:

- CO₂ emissions  
- CH₄ emissions  
- N₂O emissions  

Carbon sequestration + GHG reduction = **PBPE Climate Value**
