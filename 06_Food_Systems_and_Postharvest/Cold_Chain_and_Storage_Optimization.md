# Cold Chain and Storage Optimization (MBT55)

MBT55 enhances cold chain performance by stabilizing microbial communities,  
reducing spoilage, suppressing oxidation, and improving moisture balance  
across fresh produce, meat, dairy, and aquaculture products.  
This increases shelf life and reduces supply chain losses.

---

# 1. Overview

Cold chain failures are caused by:

- Temperature fluctuations
- Condensation and moisture imbalance
- Surface microbial growth
- Oxidation
- Poor sanitation

MBT55 provides a biological stabilization layer that protects products  
throughout storage and transport.

---

# 2. Microbial Stabilization in Cold Environments

MBT55 reduces:

- Psychrotrophic bacteria
- Cold-tolerant fungi
- Surface contamination

Mechanisms:

- Organic acids
- Niche exclusion
- Antagonism
- Redox stabilization

---

# 3. Moisture and Condensation Control

MBT55 stabilizes:

- Water activity
- Surface hydration
- Osmotic balance

Benefits:

- Reduced softening
- Lower microbial growth
- Improved texture retention

---

# 4. Oxidation Suppression

MBT55 produces:

- Quinones
- Phenolics
- Redox-active metabolites

These reduce:

- Browning
- Lipid oxidation
- Aroma degradation

---

# 5. Meat, Fish, and Dairy Stability

MBT55 improves:

- Meat color stability
- Fish freshness
- Milk microbial balance
- Egg shell hygiene

---

# 6. Storage System Integration

Effective in:

- Refrigerated warehouses
- Cold trucks
- Retail storage
- On-farm cooling systems
