# Postharvest Stability and Shelf Life (MBT55)

MBT55 improves postharvest stability by reducing spoilage, suppressing  
oxidation, stabilizing moisture, and maintaining microbial balance on  
fresh produce, grains, and animal products.  
This extends shelf life and reduces supply chain losses.

---

# 1. Overview

Postharvest losses are caused by:

- Spoilage microbes
- Oxidation
- Moisture imbalance
- Temperature stress
- Handling damage

MBT55 provides a biological stabilization system.

---

# 2. Anti-Spoilage Mechanisms

MBT55 reduces:

- Mold growth
- Soft rot
- Surface browning
- Fermentation spoilage

Mechanisms:

- Organic acids
- Antioxidant metabolites
- Niche exclusion
- Redox stabilization

---

# 3. Moisture and Hydration Control

MBT55 stabilizes:

- Water activity
- Surface hydration
- Osmotic balance

Benefits:

- Reduced softening
- Lower microbial growth
- Improved texture retention

---

# 4. Oxidation Suppression

MBT55 produces:

- Quinones
- Phenolics
- Redox-active compounds

These reduce:

- Browning
- Nutrient loss
- Aroma degradation

---

# 5. Grain and Storage Stability

MBT55 improves:

- Grain respiration stability
- Mold suppression
- Mycotoxin reduction
- Long-term storage quality

---

# 6. Cold Chain Integration

MBT55 enhances:

- Fresh produce stability
- Meat and fish shelf life
- Dairy freshness
- Egg quality

---

# 7. PBPE Integration

Postharvest stability becomes PBPE value:

- SRC（Spoilage Reduction Credit）
- QPC（Quality Premium Credit）
- FSC（Food Safety Credit）

---

# 8. One-Sentence Summary

**MBT55 extends shelf life by suppressing spoilage, stabilizing moisture,  
reducing oxidation, and maintaining microbial balance across postharvest systems.**
