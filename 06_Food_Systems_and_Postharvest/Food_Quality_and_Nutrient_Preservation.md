# Food Quality and Nutrient Preservation (MBT55)

MBT55 preserves food quality by reducing oxidation, stabilizing nutrients,  
maintaining texture, and suppressing spoilage organisms.  
This improves flavor, aroma, color, and nutritional value across fresh  
produce, grains, meat, dairy, and processed foods.

---

# 1. Overview

Nutrient loss is driven by:

- Oxidation
- Enzymatic degradation
- Microbial spoilage
- Moisture imbalance
- Temperature stress

MBT55 protects food quality through biochemical and microbial stabilization.

---

# 2. Oxidation Control

MBT55 produces:

- Quinones
- Phenolics
- Antioxidant metabolites

These reduce:

- Vitamin degradation
- Browning
- Lipid oxidation
- Aroma loss

---

# 3. Texture and Moisture Stability

MBT55 stabilizes:

- Water activity
- Cell wall integrity
- Osmotic balance

Benefits:

- Firmer fruits and vegetables
- Better grain storage quality
- Improved meat and fish texture

---

# 4. Flavor and Aroma Preservation

MBT55 reduces:

- Off-flavors
- Fermentation spoilage
- Oxidative aroma loss

This maintains natural sensory quality.

---

# 5. Nutrient Retention

MBT55 helps preserve:

- Vitamins (C, B-complex)
- Polyphenols
- Amino acids
- Carotenoids
- Essential fatty acids

Mechanisms:

- Redox stabilization
- Microbial balance
- Moisture control

---

# 6. Grain and Dry Food Quality

MBT55 improves:

- Storage stability
- Mold suppression
- Mycotoxin reduction
- Nutrient retention

---

# 7. PBPE Integration

- QPC（Quality Premium Credit）

**MBT55 preserves food quality by reducing oxidation, stabilizing nutrients,
