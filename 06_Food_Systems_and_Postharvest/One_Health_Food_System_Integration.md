# One Health Food System Integration (MBT55)

MBT55 integrates food safety, environmental health, livestock stability,  
and human well-being into a unified One Health food system.  
By reducing pathogens, stabilizing microbiomes, and improving nutrient  
preservation, MBT55 enhances safety and sustainability across the entire  
food chain.

---

# 1. What Is One Health in Food Systems?

One Health connects:

- Human health
- Animal health
- Plant health
- Soil and water ecosystems
- Climate stability

MBT55 strengthens all domains simultaneously.

---

# 2. Reduction of Foodborne Pathogens

MBT55 suppresses:

- Salmonella
- E. coli
- Listeria
- Campylobacter
- Fungal toxins

This reduces human disease risk.

---

# 3. Nutrient and Quality Preservation

MBT55 stabilizes:

- Vitamins
- Polyphenols
- Amino acids
- Carotenoids
- Essential fatty acids

This improves nutritional value.

---

# 4. Environmental Health Integration

MBT55 improves:

- Soil carbon
- Water quality
- Waste decomposition
- Odor reduction

Healthy environments → safer food.

---

# 5. Livestock and Aquaculture Integration

MBT55 enhances:

- Gut microbiome stability
- SCFA production
- Immune activation
- Pathogen suppression

This reduces AMR risk and improves food safety.

---

# 6. Supply Chain and Postharvest Integration

MBT55 reduces:

- Spoilage
- Oxidation
- Moisture imbalance
- Cold chain losses

This improves food availability and affordability.

---

# 7. PBPE Integration

One Health food system outputs become PBPE value:

- FSC（Food Safety Credit）
- QPC（Quality Premium Credit）
- SRC（Spoilage Reduction Credit）
- AMRC（AMR Reduction Credit）

---

# 8. One-Sentence Summary

**MBT55 integrates food safety, environmental health, livestock stability,  
and nutrient preservation into a unified One Health food system that  
protects human health and strengthens global food security.**
